Peek Markets Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Peek Markets ("the app") is a home screen widget that shows prices for stocks and crypto you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. The developer never receives your data.

Information stored on your device

  • Your watchlist, display settings (theme, colors, sort order) and the latest prices.
  • The API key(s) you enter for your chosen market data provider.
  • Public lists of ticker symbols, downloaded to make search work offline.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Like most Android apps, it may be included in your phone's own encrypted Android backup if you have backup turned on. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information sent to third parties

To get prices, the app connects directly from your phone to the services below. It sends only what each request needs:

  • Your chosen price provider (Twelve Data or Alpaca): the ticker symbols in your watchlist and your API key for that provider.
  • Symbol search lists: the app downloads public ticker lists from Nasdaq Trader and CoinGecko, and may ask Twelve Data's public symbol search about tickers you type. No key or personal information is sent with these.
  • Google: tapping a ticker opens a Google search or Google Finance page for it in your browser.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any internet connection, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email, contacts, location or other personal information is requested.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools are included.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with the developer or anyone else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not financial advice

Prices come from third-party providers and may be delayed or incorrect. The app is for information only and is not investment advice.
